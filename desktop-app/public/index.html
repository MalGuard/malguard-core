Privacy & Diagnostics

Device specifications and installation health may be registered with the administrator, including one notification email. Anonymous means no account identity; detailed device specifications may still be distinctive.

No automatic location lookup. Use only country and province; do not enter an address.

Turning sharing off stops uploads immediately and requests deletion. If offline, deletion remains pending until you retry. Previously delivered email cannot be recalled. Preview shows the registration payload; daily heartbeat contains only the installation ID, consent and app version.
