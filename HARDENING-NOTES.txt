MalGuard Final Security Engineering Audit v1.1.0

Security changes:
- Fixed worker-client initialization race with scan reservation.
- FREE no longer emits SAFE when official rules coverage is degraded.
- PRO no longer emits base SAFE when MultiLayerSecurity is unavailable.
- Worker result envelopes validate verdict/mode/app version.
- Engine and ArchiveEntryReader re-check actual ArrayBuffer size.
- Invalid scan modes are rejected instead of normalized.
- Added live Self-Test surface and integrity regression.
- Expanded adversarial fuzzing, worker crash/timeout/race coverage and mutation testing.

No UI redesign was performed. See FINAL-SECURITY-AUDIT.txt.
