# Code of Conduct

MalGuard contributors are expected to follow the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full Contributor Covenant is incorporated here by reference rather than reproduced.

## Our community commitments

- Be respectful, constructive and welcoming, especially to first-time contributors.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, or disclosure of private information.
- Review ideas and technical evidence rather than attacking people. Disagreement is welcome; abuse is not.
- Honor security-reporting restrictions: do not post real malware, private vulnerability details, credentials or personal data in public channels.

## Scope

These standards apply in project repositories, issues, pull requests, code review, discussions, and when representing MalGuard in public forums.

## Reporting and enforcement

For community-conduct issues, email **contact@malguard.site** with a concise description and relevant non-sensitive links. For a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Project maintainers may moderate comments, request edits, lock conversations, or restrict participation based on the severity and recurrence of conduct. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practicable; confidentiality cannot be absolutely guaranteed. No fixed response-time commitment is claimed.
